# Review rubric

Score each candidate finding against:

1. Does the behavior match the stated intent?
2. Are boundary, invalid, retry, permission, and rollback states handled?
3. Does the data shape keep ownership and invariants clear?
4. Does the check prove user-visible behavior on the matching surface?
5. Is the change limited to the named scope?

The rubric supports a verdict. It does not auto-apply a fix.
